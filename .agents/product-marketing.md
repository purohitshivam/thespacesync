# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-22

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** SpaceSync helps people find compatible people to live with through the transparent, rules-based Peace Score.
**Tagline:** "Somewhere out there is your perfect flatmate."
**What it does:** SpaceSync is a flatmate compatibility and matchmaking web application (`https://www.thespacesync.in`) focused on living habits, household boundaries, and personality compatibility rather than property listings or dating. Its signature feature is the Peace Score — an explainable, weighted compatibility estimate across Lifestyle (40%), Living Preferences (40%), and Personality & Communication (20%) with deal-breaker detection.
**Product category:** Flatmate Compatibility & Shared-Living Matchmaking Platform
**Product type:** Consumer SaaS / Two-sided Compatibility Network
**Business model:** Freemium
- **Free (₹0/month):** Full compatibility profile, Peace Score calculation, city/budget/timeline/lifestyle discovery filters, mutual interest matching, and real-time 1-to-1 messaging.
- **Premium (₹199/month):** Advanced lifestyle filters (kitchen dietary rules, pet preferences, WFH routine, overnight guest policy), deeper compatibility breakdowns, and priority visibility.

## Target Audience
**Primary segments:**
- University and MBA students (starting with Masters' Union student cohorts and campus communities)
- Young working professionals (ages 21–32)
- People relocating to major Indian metros for education, internships, or new jobs
**Geographic focus:** Bangalore, Mumbai, and Delhi NCR (Gurgaon/Noida/Delhi), while open to users across India.
**Primary use case:** Finding a genuinely compatible flatmate before signing a lease together or filling a room in a shared flat.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Verify day-to-day living compatibility (sleep schedule, cleanliness, kitchen dietary boundaries, smoking, guests, budget) before committing to a 11-month lease and security deposit.
- Avoid awkward post-move-in conflicts over chores, noise, overnight visitors, or shared expenses.
- Connect privately and safely without exposing personal phone numbers on public Facebook/Telegram/WhatsApp groups.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Most shared-living mismatches happen not because the flat was bad, but because two people with incompatible daily routines, kitchen boundaries, guest policies, or noise tolerances moved in together blindly.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Property portals & broker groups (NoBroker, MagicBricks, Flat & Flatmates Facebook groups):** Focus on rooms and brokerage fees, ignore human compatibility, and expose personal phone numbers to spam.
- **Unstructured WhatsApp/Telegram spreadsheets:** Lack structured lifestyle/deal-breaker questions, making it impossible to compare 20+ candidates objectively.
**What it costs them:** Lost security deposits, broken leases, months of daily domestic friction, sleep deprivation, and stress at home.
**Emotional tension:** Anxiety about moving to a new city and getting stuck in a tense, uncomfortable home environment.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Transparent, Deterministic Peace Score:** Weighted across Lifestyle (40%), Living Preferences (40%), and Personality & Communication (20%), with low/medium/high importance multipliers and explicit deal-breaker flags.
- **Complementary vs. Identical Recognition:** Recognises when differences are healthy (e.g., balanced socialisers vs. independent recharge styles) while flagging true deal-breaker clashes.
- **Privacy-First & Zero Dummy Data:** Profiles remain unpublished drafts until explicitly published; personal contact details stay private until mutual match; strictly zero fabricated profiles or fake social proof.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Why not just use Flat & Flatmates Facebook groups or WhatsApp?" | Those groups show rooms, not how someone lives day-to-day — and require posting your phone number publicly. SpaceSync compares 20+ living habits privately before you ever chat. |
| "How is the Peace Score calculated? Is it a black-box AI guess?" | Never a black box. Every Peace Score shows the exact category breakdown (Lifestyle 40%, Living 40%, Personality 20%), data completeness percentage, top alignments, and any deal-breaker conflicts. |
| "What if there aren't many profiles in my neighbourhood yet?" | SpaceSync never pads discovery with fake profiles. When an orbit is quiet, we show an honest empty state and let you invite your cohort or join the city launch list. |

**Anti-persona:** Property brokers, commercial PG operators, short-term vacation subletters, or anyone looking for a dating app.
